Adaptive Detection and Mitigation of Liquid Rocket Engine Failures

This tentative pitch builds on my thesis work on launch vehicle propulsion failures. The idea is to combine a physics-based model of liquid rocket engine failure propagation with a stochastic detection layer that flags anomalous behavior early, then feed that information into an adaptive controller that adjusts vehicle trajectory in response. For this course, the plan is to prototype a simplified version of this pipeline — likely a reduced-order engine model, a basic anomaly detector, and a simple adaptive control loop — as a proof of concept rather than a full end-to-end system.
