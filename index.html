Hi, I'm Menna Hussein

PhD Candidate, Engineering Systems Lab @ MIT

PhD candidate at the Engineering Systems Lab at MIT. My research focuses on launch vehicle propulsion failures, integrating physics-based modeling with stochastic detection methods and adaptive control to improve system reliability. I got my BSc in Aerospace Engineering from Arizona State University in 2020, and I worked at the Egyptian Space Agency, Benchmark Space Systems, and GE Aerospace Research.
