What I Study

Research Interests

My work focuses on understanding, predicting, and mitigating failures in launch vehicle and liquid rocket engine systems, with an emphasis on adaptive control strategies.

🛰️

Launch Vehicle Failures

Investigating root causes, failure modes, and system-level impacts of anomalies during launch vehicle ascent and operation.

🔥

Liquid Rocket Engine Failure Propagation

Modeling how failures originate and propagate through liquid rocket engine subsystems to better anticipate cascading effects.

🛡️

Mitigation Strategies

Developing methods to detect and mitigate failure conditions before they escalate into mission-critical events.

🎯

Adaptive Trajectory Control

Designing adaptive control approaches that adjust vehicle trajectory in real time in response to detected failures.
